# Datasets

No accuracy claim is made. No word-error-rate number is in this file. A lift is not said to improve anything until it beats the base model on held-out clips it was not tuned on.

Planned sets, licenses only. None of these were downloaded or scored in this build.

- Mozilla Common Voice. CC0. Accent tags. Not run.
- CORAAL. Regional African American English. License is the corpus terms at the time of use. Not run.
- Speech Accent Archive. License must be checked per clip before use. Not run.
- Linguistic Atlas collections. Use only where the license allows. Not run.

Louisiana Creole has thin open data. The Gulf Coast lift is marked experimental until sourced audio exists.

The on-device speech pack was not confirmed on a real phone.
